The Problem

The Meter Is Running, But You Can't Find Where The Water's Going?

A rising water bill with no obvious cause, a damp patch that won't dry out, or a water meter that keeps spinning even with everything in the house turned off. These are the calls we get most, and they almost always mean a leak that isn't visible from the surface.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Leak Detection

  • 1
    Your water bill jumped with no change in usage. A hidden leak is the most common explanation once the obvious causes are ruled out.
  • 2
    The water meter keeps moving with everything off. This is the single clearest confirmation something is leaking between the meter and the house.
  • 3
    A warm or damp patch on the floor, which often points to a leak under the slab specifically.
  • 4
    A hissing sound near a wall or under a floor when nothing is running.

Any of this sound familiar? Call (480) 741-9332 and we'll find it.

Why Equipment Matters More Than Guesswork

A leak that's found by opening walls or digging in the wrong spot costs real money in repair and restoration that a precise diagnosis avoids entirely. Acoustic equipment listens for the specific frequency water makes escaping a pressurized line, and combined with moisture readings, it typically narrows a leak's location to a small, specific area rather than a general zone. That's the difference between opening one section of drywall and opening three.

We also check whether a leak is on the supply side, meaning it's pressurized water actively being pushed out, or the drain side, meaning it's wastewater escaping without pressure behind it. That distinction changes both the urgency and the repair approach, and it's one of the first things we determine on-site.

Commercial properties call us for this too, most often a business that suspects a leak somewhere in its plumbing but doesn't have the equipment or expertise in-house to locate it without opening up finished space unnecessarily.

Irrigation And Pool Leaks Too

Not every leak we find is inside the home. A lot of Queen Creek properties run drip irrigation or a full sprinkler system. A break in one of those lines can drive up a water bill just as much as a leak inside the house, sometimes more, since irrigation runs on a timer without anyone watching it. If your property has a pool, the same acoustic equipment can help locate a leak in pool plumbing specifically, separate from the pool shell itself.
