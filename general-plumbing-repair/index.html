What This Covers

Common General Repairs

  • 1
    A leaking or stuck shut-off valve. These often corrode or seize over time and can fail exactly when you need them most, like during another repair.
  • 2
    A dripping angle stop under a sink or toilet. Small, but worth fixing before it stains a cabinet floor or drips into a wall.
  • 3
    An outdoor hose bib or irrigation valve that won't stop dripping. Summer heat here breaks down rubber washers and seals faster than in milder climates, so this is one of our most common general calls.
  • 4
    A loose or leaking fixture connection at a sink, tub spout, or supply line that isn't a full fixture failure, just a fitting that needs resealing or tightening properly.

Why A General Call Still Gets A Real Diagnosis

Even for a small job, our technicians look at the fixture or fitting in context, not in isolation. A shut-off valve that's failed once is worth checking against the others nearby, since they're often the same age and material and tend to fail in the same window. A dripping angle stop that seems minor can point to water pressure running higher than it should sitewide, which is worth flagging even if it isn't the reason you called.
