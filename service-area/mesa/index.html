Where Southeast Mesa Meets Queen Creek

ZIP 85212 lists both Queen Creek ZIP codes, 85142 and 85140, among its neighbors, and it is served by the Gilbert, Higley, and Queen Creek school districts. That overlap is a good picture of how mixed the boundary is here.

Communities in this corner include Eastmark, Santa Rita Ranch, Boulder Creek, and Mountain Ranch. Most are newer subdivisions with parks, trails, and plenty of landscaping. That landscaping means irrigation lines, hose bibs, and backflow assemblies that homeowners rarely think about until one leaks.

The Eastmark Pattern

Eastmark is a master-planned community built on the site of the former General Motors Desert Proving Ground, and it is anchored by the 96-acre Eastmark Great Park. Most homes there are recent construction, so the plumbing story is about new systems rather than old ones.

Newer homes use PEX supply lines, builder-grade water heaters, and subdivision water pressure that can run higher than fixtures prefer. Those are the issues we look at first when an Eastmark homeowner calls.

Mesa also runs its own water utility. According to the City's published water quality reports, hardness varies by source and area, so we check your address before sizing a softener.

Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

  • 1
    Shut off your main water valve, usually near the water meter or a street-side hose bib, to stop water flow immediately.
  • 2
    Cut power to any wet area at the breaker if water is near an outlet, switch, or appliance.
  • 3
    Photograph the damage before cleanup if your home is still under a builder warranty.

    Call our 24/7 line and give us your street address so we can confirm coverage and send a technician.
