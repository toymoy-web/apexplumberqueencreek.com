A Border Area, Not One Single Neighborhood

San Tan Valley itself is a separate, distinct community from Queen Creek, set against the San Tan Mountains foothills with a genuine mix of golf, age-restricted, and everyday family neighborhoods spread across a large, still-growing area of Pinal County. Queen Creek's own town boundary has shifted over the years too. The Ironwood Crossing neighborhood, for example, was formally annexed into Queen Creek's town limits back in 2018, despite sitting in this same general border area near San Tan Valley.

What that means practically, in plain terms: our coverage here isn't a single tidy neighborhood the way Encanterra or The Villages are. It's a real, growing border area, and the honest answer to "do you cover my street" is usually yes, but it's worth a quick confirmation call given how fast new development continues here.

Confirm coverage for your address: Call (480) 741-9332.

Growth That Keeps Redrawing Boundaries

San Tan Valley grew from around 81,000 residents in 2010 to well over 100,000 in the years since, one of the fastest growth rates in the state, and that pace has shown no real sign of slowing down. New master-planned communities continue breaking ground across the wider area every year, which means the practical line between "Queen Creek" and "San Tan Valley" keeps shifting as annexations and new development happen year over year, community by community, street by street. We'd honestly rather you call and ask than try guessing based on an old map.

Real Communities In This Area

Some real, established communities like Ironwood Crossing, Johnson Ranch, Pecan Creek, and Skyline Ranch sit in the area, each with its own mix of housing ages and styles. We don't treat this stretch as one single undifferentiated zone. A call from a newer Ironwood Crossing home gets assessed on its own terms, separate from an older property elsewhere in the same general area. That's the same approach we take across every part of our service area, not just this one, whether a property sits inside Queen Creek's limits or just across the line.
