New Construction

Brand-New Homes: Check The Warranty First

Most homes in Barney Farms are very new, so the first question is usually warranty. Builder warranties on plumbing generally cover a limited period, and your paperwork spells out what is covered and who must do the work. A repair by an outside plumber could affect a warranty claim, so call your builder first when a problem may be covered.

New homes can still have plumbing problems. A leaking supply connection, a toilet that keeps running, or a water heater that will not light can happen at any age. If the problem is not covered, or the warranty period has ended, call us and we will tell you honestly what we find.

Move-In Checks

Day-One Plumbing Checks For New Owners

Find your main water shutoff and make sure everyone in the house knows where it is. Check under sinks for drips after the first few weeks of use. Write down the water heater make, model, and install date so you can track its age.

These checks take a few minutes and save time in an emergency. If you find a drip or a damp cabinet, call your builder if the home is under warranty, or call us if it is not.

Before We Arrive

If Water Is Running At Your Barney Farms Home

If water is running or pooling, these steps limit the damage while you wait for a technician.

  • 1
    Close the water. Turn the stop valve under the fixture clockwise, or close the main shutoff for the house.
  • 2
    Check your warranty. If the home is under a builder warranty, note the problem and call the builder as well.
  • 3
    Protect the water heater. If the leak is near the heater, switch off its breaker or set a gas unit's control to off.
  • 4
    Document the damage. Photograph wet areas for your records and move belongings away from the water.
