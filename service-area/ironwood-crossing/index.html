About Ironwood Crossing

Wikipedia's Queen Creek entry lists Ironwood Crossing as a neighborhood annexed in 2018 and notes that the annexation extended town limits into Pinal County. On February 21, 2018, the Queen Creek Town Council unanimously approved pre-annexation development agreements that named Fulton Homes' Ironwood Crossing, according to Rose Law Group Reporter.

Listing data shows homes here built from 2004 to 2023 by Fulton Homes and Woodside Homes, in ZIP 85140, in Pinal County. Gilbert Sun News described a Fulton sales office at Ironwood and Ocotillo roads, which gives a useful anchor for where the community sits.

Before We Arrive

If Water Is Running At Your Ironwood Crossing Home

If water is running or pooling, these steps limit the damage while you wait for a technician.

  • 1
    Close the water. Turn the stop valve under the fixture clockwise, or close the main shutoff for the house.
  • 2
    Use the house shutoff. The valve at the street meter is usually for the utility, so use the shutoff at the house first.
  • 3
    Protect the water heater. If the leak is near the heater, switch off its breaker or set a gas unit's control to off.
  • 4
    Document the damage. Photograph wet areas for your records and move belongings away from the water.
