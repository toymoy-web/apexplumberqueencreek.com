Two Towns, Two Water Systems

Gilbert runs its own water system and publishes its own annual water quality report, separate from the Town of Queen Creek's. According to each town's own published water quality report, hardness and mineral content can differ from one side of the boundary to the other, and from one neighborhood to the next.

That matters when you are choosing a softener or sizing a water heater. We ask for your address, check the water report that applies to it, and recommend equipment for the water you actually have.
