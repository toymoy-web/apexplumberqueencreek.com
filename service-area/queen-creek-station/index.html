About Queen Creek Station

The Arizona Republic reported on March 13, 2013 that the Queen Creek Town Council gave its first approvals for Queen Creek Station. The 1,139-acre plan from Fulton Homes covers the northeast and northwest corners of Ellsworth and Queen Creek roads. Council approved a preliminary plat for 677 lots and design review for eight floor plans in three architectural styles.

That article also described a mixed-use area with offices, retail, and higher-density housing, plus lower-density housing near the Ellsworth Suburban Mini-farms. Listings we reviewed show homes here built between 2015 and 2020, in ZIP 85142.

The old Rittenhouse Elementary School, built in 1925 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1998, stands at the same intersection and now houses the San Tan Historical Society, according to Wikipedia. Exact residential boundaries are not published in the sources we reviewed.

Home Age

A Community Planned In 2013

Listings show homes here built from 2015 to 2020, so many are now between six and eleven years old. At that age, a first replacement of a worn part is a normal expense, not a sign of a bad house. Toilet fill valves, faucet cartridges, and garbage disposals are common first items.

A water heater in this age range is worth a look too. The U.S. Department of Energy lists 10 to 15 years as typical storage tank life. A technician can check the relief valve, the fittings, and signs of sediment before a small issue becomes a leak.

Water Quality

Pre-Plumbed For Soft Water: What To Know

A NewHomeSource listing for The Reserve at Queen Creek Station says its homes came with plumbing pre-installed for soft water and reverse osmosis. Whether your home has that depends on the model and the options the first owner chose. A technician can tell by looking at the plumbing near the water heater and under the kitchen sink.

If your home is set up that way, adding a system can be simpler than starting from scratch. We test your tap first and explain what a system would change. See our water softener installation and water filtration pages for details.

Before We Arrive

If Water Is Running At Your Queen Creek Station Home

If water is running or pooling, these steps limit the damage while you wait for a technician.

  • 1
    Close the water. Turn the stop valve under the fixture clockwise, or close the main shutoff for the house.
  • 2
    Stop an overflowing toilet. Lift the tank lid and lift the float, or close the stop valve on the wall behind the toilet.
  • 3
    Protect the water heater. If the leak is near the heater, switch off its breaker or set a gas unit's control to off.
  • 4
    Document the damage. Photograph wet areas for your records and move belongings away from the water.
