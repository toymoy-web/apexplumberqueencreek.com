Shower & Bathtub Installation in Queen Creek, AZ

Tub-To-Shower Conversions

One of the most requested jobs we do, usually for accessibility reasons as homeowners plan to stay in their homes longer, or simply because a walk-in shower fits how the household actually uses the bathroom. The plumbing side involves relocating or reworking the valve and drain to match the new layout, which is where a conversion differs from a simple fixture swap. We coordinate the plumbing timeline with whoever is installing the surround or base if that's not us directly.

Fixture Choices Worth Knowing About

A pressure-balancing or thermostatic valve, now standard on most new installations, keeps water temperature steady even if someone flushes a toilet or runs another fixture elsewhere in the house, which matters more than people expect until they've lived with the older kind that doesn't. For a walk-in conversion specifically, a linear or center drain changes the slope and framing requirements slightly, worth deciding early since it affects the base installation, not just the finish.

Ready to talk through your project? Call (480) 741-9332.

Bathtub Or Shower: Which Makes Sense For Your Household

Removing the only tub in a home can affect resale value for buyers specifically looking for one, particularly families with young children, so it's worth thinking about beyond just current household preference if you plan to sell within the next several years. A second bathroom with a tub elsewhere in the house changes that calculation considerably, since it's less likely to matter to a future buyer if there's already a tub somewhere on the property. We're happy to talk through that trade-off honestly rather than just taking the job as requested.

For a straight upgrade rather than a conversion, a like-for-like tub or shower replacement is a simpler, faster job. It still gives you updated fixtures and a valve that meets current code, without the framing changes a full conversion involves, which keeps both cost and timeline down.

Have a shower or tub project in mind? Call (480) 741-9332 for a real quote.

What We Check Before Any Installation

Before starting an installation, we confirm the existing valve and drain rough-in match what the new fixture needs, since not every shower or tub uses the same connection spacing, and older fixtures in particular sometimes don't match current standard measurements. We also check the supply line's condition behind the wall while we have access, which is worth knowing about before closing everything back up rather than discovering an issue on a future unrelated repair.

Worth Knowing

Hard Water And New Fixtures

A brand-new shower valve and glass will start showing the same hard-water spotting and scale as everything else in a Queen Creek home within a matter of months, since it's the water itself causing it, not the fixture quality. If that's a frustration you've had before, it's worth asking us about a water softener at the same time as your installation.
